Coach: Thanks for joining today. How has the job search been going since our last session?

Candidate: Honestly, a bit slow. I applied to about 12 data analyst roles on MyCareersFuture but only got one reply, and it was a rejection. I'm starting to wonder if my resume is the problem.

Coach: Let's look at that. You completed the SCTP Data Analytics programme in March, and before that you were in retail operations for 9 years, correct?

Candidate: Yes, last role was Assistant Operations Manager at a supermarket chain. I was retrenched in November when they restructured.

Coach: In the programme, which tools did you get strongest in?

Coach: That capstone is actually very relevant — retail domain plus analytics. Is it on your resume?

Candidate: Only one line. I didn't think it counted since it wasn't a real job.

Coach: It counts. Employers hiring career switchers look for applied evidence. What about salary expectations?

Candidate: I was earning $4,800 before. I know I may need to come down for an entry analyst role. I can accept $3,800, minimum $3,500. I can start immediately since I'm not working.

Candidate: One more thing — I get very nervous in interviews. In the one phone screen I had, I went blank when they asked me to describe a difficult stakeholder situation.

Coach: We'll work on that. You have 9 years of real stakeholder stories from retail — we just need to structure them. Let's agree on actions: I'll review your resume by Friday and you'll expand the capstone section with the dashboard outcomes. You'll draft 3 STAR stories from your retail experience before our next session. And I'll shortlist 5 suitable junior data analyst openings, including from our hiring partners.

Candidate: Okay, that sounds good. Thank you.

Coach: Great. Same time next Tuesday.
